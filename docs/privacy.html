1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, encryption, and protection of candidate personal data for the mobile application Engineering Officer BY MH (Package ID: com.engineeringofficer.civil), owned and operated by PRIME MULTI SERVICES AND SUPPLIERS ("we", "us", or "our").

2. What Candidate Data We Collect

We only collect the minimal data necessary to deliver examination preparation and test analytics:

  • Account Identifiers: Name, email address, and preparation targets (MPSC MES, SSC JE, PWD, WRD, ZP, RRB JE).
  • Academic Telemetry: MCQs solved, scores, accuracy percentage, time per question, and saved notes in Mistake Notebook.
  • Transaction Identifiers: Razorpay Payment ID and subscription dates. We do not store banking passwords, CVV, or card numbers.

3. Third-Party Integrations & Zero Data Selling Guarantee

We treat student privacy with the highest engineering rigor. We DO NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade candidate personal data to advertisers, telemarketers, or unauthorized third parties.

  • Razorpay: RBI authorized and PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway for secure payment handling.
  • Supabase / PostgreSQL: Secure cloud database with TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Google AI: Used strictly for generating educational MCQs and step-by-step civil engineering explanations. No student personally identifiable information (PII) is ever transmitted to AI models.
